Every multi-GPU vLLM compose auto-enables P2P from its own entrypoint — launcher AND raw 'docker compose' alike — so a capable driver plus a P2P-off container usually means one of: NVLINK_MODE=force_off is set (.env or environment), the container predates the running compose (recreate it), or this engine's compose doesn't do interconnect detection at all (the llama.cpp-family and SGLang composes don't).
